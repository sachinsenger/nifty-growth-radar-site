How the desk should be used

  1. Check market regime and index structure first.
  2. For swing trades, require trend alignment, relative strength, acceptable RSI and a sensible support/stop structure.
  3. Read the catalyst separately: a sector headline is not a company-specific catalyst.
  4. For option buying, wait for live underlying confirmation; PCR/OI is context, not a standalone signal.
  5. Use a fixed rupee risk budget and calculate quantity from the stop distance.
  6. Skip setups where the stop is too wide, liquidity is poor, or the thesis depends on an unverified headline.

Due diligence: Verify current price, corporate announcements, earnings, liquidity, derivatives data and broker execution conditions before trading. This dashboard is research/education and not personalized investment advice.
