🌍 What is driving the market?

  • Oil & geopolitics — Negative: Widening Iran conflict pushed crude higher, with Brent around $96.6/bbl. Higher crude is a key risk for India because it can pressure inflation, the trade balance and corporate margins.
  • US yields & dollar — Negative: US 10Y yields were around 4.8% and the dollar remained firm. Higher US yields can reduce the relative attractiveness of emerging-market equities and keep FII sentiment cautious.
  • India growth — Positive: Q1 FY27 GDP growth was 7.8%, while private investment/capex showed improvement. This provides a fundamental cushion despite the external risk environment.
  • Institutional flows — Supportive: FIIs bought roughly ₹1,143 crore and DIIs bought roughly ₹1,847 crore on 1 Sep. DII support is helping absorb foreign selling pressure.
